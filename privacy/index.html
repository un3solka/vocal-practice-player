Vocal Practice Player

Privacy Policy

Updated November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Vocal Practice Player, developed by Maxim Karonchyk, handles information. The app does not require an account of its own.

Data stored on your device

The app stores local copies of imported audio, track names and durations, library order, playback position, speed and pitch settings, loop settings, saved segments, lyrics, and the artist and title saved for a track. It also stores whether you completed the introduction, how many free practice sessions you have used, and the last verified status of your Plus access.

The app uses this data to play your audio, save your practice, and determine which features are available. The developer has no server that stores or remotely accesses your library. The app has no cloud sync of its own. Apple’s device backups and transfers may include app data, depending on your system settings.

Optional lyrics search

When you request a search, the app sends the song title and artist to LRCLIB over HTTPS. An exact-match search also sends the track duration. The request identifies the app and its version, and LRCLIB can receive the IP address used to connect. The request does not include your audio, manually entered lyrics, saved segments, or library.

LRCLIB handles requests on its own systems. The developer does not control its logs or retention practices. You can play audio, use saved lyrics, and enter lyrics manually without contacting LRCLIB.

Plus purchases

Apple processes subscription purchases, renewals, and restores through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to read and verify transaction and subscription status for Plus and may retain verified access details on your device. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics, and the website

The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send its own usage telemetry to the developer. Apple services such as App Store, TestFlight, and device diagnostics operate separately under Apple’s policies and your settings.

This website has no analytics scripts or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process connection information such as your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you change it or delete it. Deleting a track removes its library entry, settings, saved segments, and lyrics. The app also attempts to remove its local audio copy. Your original file in Files stays untouched. If the app detects a damaged library, it may keep a local recovery copy that can include earlier library information and lyrics.

Deleting the app removes its local container. Offloading the app keeps app data. Apple manages any existing device backups separately. The developer cannot remotely restore or erase your local library.

Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, email maksim.karonchyk@gmail.com. If you email the developer, they receive your email address and the information you include and use it to respond to your request. Please send only what is needed to handle the request.
