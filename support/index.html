How do I add a song?

Tap + in the library and choose audio from Files. The app keeps a local copy, so playback does not depend on the original file staying in the same place. Keep your originals as a separate backup.

How do I repeat a phrase?

Open A–B segments, set the start A and end B, then turn on repeat. Without repeat, playback stops at B. You can save a segment for later and adjust speed and pitch independently.

Why can’t I find lyrics?

Lyrics search needs an internet connection and uses LRCLIB. Check the artist and title or try again later. You can also enter lyrics manually; saved lyrics work offline.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Open Settings → Plus in the app and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. To manage or cancel, choose Manage subscription when Plus is active, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewal; access remains until the paid period ends. For billing or refunds, visit Apple’s purchase support.

Where is my library? How do I delete it?

Your library is stored on this device with no app cloud sync. Delete a track in the library to remove its entry, saved segments and lyrics; the app also attempts to delete its local audio copy. The original file in Files stays untouched. Deleting the app removes its local data. Offloading keeps app data, and system backups are managed separately through Apple.

What should I include in a support email?

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue and the error message, if any. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment details or private audio files.

We use your email address and the information you send to respond to your request. For more information about app data, read Privacy policy in the app’s Settings.
