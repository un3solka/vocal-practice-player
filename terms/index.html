Using your music and lyrics

The app helps you practice with imported audio, speed and pitch adjustments, A–B segments and lyrics. Only import or use content you have the right to use. Importing audio or finding lyrics does not grant copyright or redistribution rights.

Free features and Plus

Importing, playback, speed, pitch and A–B segments are free. The free version includes three practice sessions per installation and lyrics for up to three songs at a time. Plus unlocks unlimited practice sessions and adding lyrics without that limit. Saved lyrics remain available to read and edit after Plus ends.

Plus is offered as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription through Apple. The purchase screen shows the current localized price and billing period before confirmation. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; renewal is charged within the final 24 hours of that period.

Manage or cancel in the app’s subscription screen or Apple Account subscription settings. Restore purchases in the app using the Apple Account used for purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests; see Apple’s purchase support.

Local library and external services

Your audio and saved lyrics are stored locally. The app has no account or cloud synchronization of its own. Keep original audio files separately: deleting the app removes its local library, and the developer cannot restore it remotely.

Optional lyrics search uses LRCLIB and sends the artist, title and, for exact matching, track duration. Audio is not uploaded. Results and service availability are not guaranteed; manual lyrics entry works without that service. The in-app privacy policy provides further details.
